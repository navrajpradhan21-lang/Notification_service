## Dead-letter-queue

Lesson 4 — Dead Letter Queue (DLQ)
Imagine Gmail rejects an email because the recipient's email address is invalid.
Our current BullMQ configuration retries the job three times:
1. Attempt 1 → Failed.
2. Attempt 2 → Failed.
3. Attempt 3 → Failed.
The job remains in BullMQ's failed state because we configured removeOnFail: false

But how do we manage these permanently failed jobs?
That's where a Dead Letter Queue (DLQ) comes in.
What is a Dead Letter Queue?
A DLQ is a separate queue used to store jobs that have exhausted their retry attempts
or cannot be processed successfully.
Notification Queue

Normal email jobs -----> Notification Worker(Attempts delivery up to 3 times)------->All attempts failedDead Letter Queue
Stores permanently failed jobs for investigation or replay   <-----------------------------

Why is this useful?
- Failed jobs don't disappear.
- Developers can investigate failures.
- Jobs can be retried after fixing the problem.
- You can monitor permanent failures
